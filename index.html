About Me

The story so far.

I'm Dhairya Arora, born and brought up in Faridabad, with a deep curiosity for how technology shapes the world around us. Today, I'm pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Science with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence at Newton School of Technology, affiliated with Rishihood University, Sonipat, Haryana.

What sets me apart is the breadth of my interests. I don't fit neatly into one box — and I consider that a strength. My world is organized around three passions: academics and technology, sports and fitness, and performing arts. I believe that a well-rounded perspective fuels better thinking, stronger creativity, and more resilient problem-solving.

My goal is clear: I want to become a developer and entrepreneur — someone who doesn't just write code but creates meaningful products, builds companies, and makes a tangible impact. I'm at the beginning of that journey, and I'm committed to learning with intention, building with purpose, and growing every single day.
